## Description

Omni helps you explore your day through astrology, conversation and personal reflection.

CHAT ABOUT YOUR STARS
Ask about your daily horoscope, love, work or style. Omni AI asks for your Sun sign or birthday when needed and can use the relevant details you choose to save. General sign readings are symbolic interpretations; chat does not calculate an exact natal chart or live planetary transits.

EXPLORE COSMOS
Draw three distinct tarot cards from a 78-card deck and bring your actual draw into an AI conversation. Choose your sign and occasion for a daily outfit palette, then create a share card on your device.

MEMORY YOU CONTROL
With Plus, choose what Omni remembers, review suggested notes, and edit, delete or export your records. Temporary conversations exclude saved memories. Online conversations require Apple sign-in and your permission to share relevant content with OpenAI. Optional account sync asks separately.

KEEP YOUR DAILY RITUALS
Use your private journal, daily check-ins and guided connection reflections. No Omni account is required for on-device features or subscribing. Plus adds online AI conversations, memory and additional reflection features. Existing users keep their records.

Astrology and tarot are for entertainment and personal reflection, not guaranteed predictions or medical, legal or financial advice. For adults 18 and older.

Omni Plus is an auto-renewing subscription. Payment is charged to your Apple Account. Renewal occurs unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your App Store account settings.

## Review notes

This update preserves all version 1.0 on-device features, guest subscription purchase/restore, existing product IDs and user records. New Cosmos is available from Today. Daily outfits, local share cards and the 78-card draw work without a subscription. AI interpretation opens a draft in Talk and never sends automatically.

Talk requires optional Sign in with Apple, an active Plus subscription, and explicit OpenAI conversation consent. Buying/restoring Plus itself never requires an Omni account. Existing guest purchases can be linked after optional sign-in without repurchasing. To test online readings: subscribe or restore, sign in with Apple, open Talk, choose a starter or type a question, tap Send, allow online conversations, then tap Send again. The production service supports both Production and Apple Sandbox receipts.

Saved birth/style details are optional, explicitly confirmed Memory notes. Temporary conversations exclude saved notes. AI responses are symbolic and do not claim exact ephemeris calculations. The user can manage/delete/export memory or delete their account in-app. No public dating, contact import, Google sign-in or generated AI image service is offered in this release; those unfinished additions are hidden.

Keep the approved US availability, 18+ rating, $7.99 monthly and $39.99 yearly prices, and manual release after approval. New privacy fields should be reviewed against actual final data handling, not guessed.
